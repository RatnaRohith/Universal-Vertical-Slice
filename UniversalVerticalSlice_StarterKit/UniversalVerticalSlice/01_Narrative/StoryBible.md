# Story Bible — Signal at Kepler-9

## Logline
When an orbital relay station begins broadcasting a distress call from a future timestamp, a lone field operator must restore its power and transmit a warning before the station falls out of orbit, while a damaged security system mistakes the operator for an intruder.

## Premise
Kepler-9 is an aging relay station above a storm-wrapped moon. Its crew vanished during a communications blackout. The station's beacon now repeats a short warning that appears to originate several minutes in the future.

## Player character
- Role: Field operator sent to inspect the relay
- Immediate goal: Restore auxiliary power and transmit the warning
- Motivation: Prevent the signal from being lost and protect a nearby survey vessel
- Constraints: Limited equipment; no full armory; station systems are unreliable
- Voice: Calm, practical, concise

## Supporting voice
- Relay AI: Damaged station assistant. Speaks in clipped diagnostic fragments.
- Survey Control: Remote contact heard over radio. Limited to two short transmissions.

## World rules
- Station gravity is stable in the playable area.
- Emergency power supports doors, lights, and one communications console.
- The security drone reacts to movement/noise and can be avoided or disabled through a local power reroute.
- The mysterious signal is a narrative hook, not fully explained in this slice.

## Narrative themes
- Trust in imperfect information
- Responsibility under uncertainty
- Technology outliving its creators

## Tone
Quiet tension, industrial sci-fi, restrained dialogue, environmental storytelling. Avoid constant exposition.

## Story beats
1. Arrival: the station looks abandoned but its beacon is active.
2. Discovery: the distress call's timestamp is impossible.
3. Complication: auxiliary power is offline and a security drone patrols the corridor.
4. Choice of approach: avoid the drone or disable its route by rerouting power.
5. Climax: transmit the warning from the relay chamber.
6. Hook: the response contains the player's own voice, recorded several minutes ahead.

## Environmental storytelling
- Scratched warning near the power panel
- A floating tool and a damaged tether in the airlock
- Repeated timestamp on a flickering status screen
- Drone path marked by warning lights
- Final console displays an incoming transmission with the operator's call sign

## Content boundaries
No gore required. Tension comes from sound, lighting, constrained space, and the threat of failure.
