# Character Briefs

## Field Operator
- Function: Player avatar
- Silhouette: Practical EVA suit, helmet, compact utility pack
- Palette: Off-white/charcoal suit with cyan status lights and amber warning details
- Required animations: idle, walk, sprint, turn, interact, jump/land, hit/stagger or detection reaction
- Audio: suit movement, footsteps, interaction exertion optional
- Gameplay needs: readable hands/interaction pose; camera must not clip through walls

## Relay AI
- Function: Diegetic tutorial and exposition
- Delivery: Radio/console voice, no full-body character
- Performance: Calm, degraded, short lines
- Required lines: welcome/arrival, power diagnostic, final anomaly
- Implementation: subtitle text and voice can be independently enabled

## Security Drone
- Function: One pressure/stealth encounter
- Silhouette: Compact maintenance drone with one bright sensor
- States: Patrol → Investigate → Pursue → Search/Return
- Feedback: Servo SFX, sensor color change, clear detection warning
