# ART — Art Requirements and Direction

## Visual target
Grounded stylized-realistic industrial sci-fi: readable shapes, controlled detail, cool ambient fill, cyan system indicators, amber warnings, restrained emissive use.

## Composition rules
- One dominant landmark per space.
- Interactive panels use consistent shape language and accent colors.
- Avoid visual noise near the objective and interaction prompt.
- Use decals to imply age, maintenance, and emergency response.
- Keep critical routes readable under gameplay camera conditions.

## Environment kit
- Floor, wall, ceiling modules
- Door frames and doors
- Cable trays, pipes, vents
- Panels, consoles, warning signage
- Light fixtures, emergency strips
- Damaged props and debris
- Exterior star/moon view cards or skybox

## Asset quality tiers
- Hero: relay console, power panel, security drone
- Standard: doors, wall modules, lights
- Background: cables, small clutter, distant exterior details

## Art review checklist
- Scale and silhouette read at gameplay distance
- Material response supports lighting
- No texture stretching or UV errors
- Emissive values do not wash out nearby surfaces
- Collision and visual meshes align
- Color theme preserves gameplay readability
