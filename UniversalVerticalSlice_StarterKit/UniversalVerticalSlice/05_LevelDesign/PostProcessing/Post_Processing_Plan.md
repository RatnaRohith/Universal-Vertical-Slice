# Post-Processing Plan

## Baseline
- Tone mapping and exposure: establish first; avoid auto-exposure pumping.
- Color grading: cool shadows, neutral highlights, controlled cyan accents.
- Bloom: low-to-moderate; only strong emissives should bloom.
- Ambient occlusion: subtle contact depth without dirty halos.
- Fog: optional thin atmospheric depth in long corridors.
- Depth of field: off during normal gameplay; optional authored ending shot.
- Motion blur: optional and user-disableable.
- Film grain/vignette: very subtle or omitted if it reduces readability.
- Anti-aliasing/upscaling: choose per engine and target platform.

## Acceptance
- Objective text, interactables, hazards, and enemy silhouettes remain readable.
- No severe exposure shifts while turning the camera.
- Effects can be reduced/disabled where practical for accessibility.
- Performance is measured with the final combination of lighting, VFX, and post effects.
