# Lighting Plan — LVL-001

## Airlock
- Cool, low-intensity ambient fill.
- One brighter light near the exit to guide movement.
- Emergency strip lighting establishes direction.
- Keep the player silhouette readable against the exterior view.

## Service Corridor
- Repeating practical lights establish rhythm.
- Flicker only at authored moments; do not use random flicker on critical route cues.
- Amber warning fixtures mark power problems.
- Drone sensor and alert light must remain visible without overpowering the scene.

## Relay Chamber
- Console is the key focal point with cyan emissive accents.
- Use a subtle back/rim light to separate the player from the background.
- Final transmission adds a controlled light pulse and brief audio cue.
- Avoid excessive bloom that hides console labels.

## Technical checklist
- Choose real-time versus baked lighting according to engine and target.
- Set exposure and tone mapping before fine-tuning material colors.
- Test shadow quality and flicker in motion.
- Verify emergency lights do not imply a false route.
- Profile in a packaged build at target resolution.
