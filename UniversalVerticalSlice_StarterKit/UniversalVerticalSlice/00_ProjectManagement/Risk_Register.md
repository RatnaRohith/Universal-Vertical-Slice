# Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RSK-001 | Scope expands beyond one mission | Medium | High | Keep out-of-scope list visible; defer nonessential features | New feature adds another system/level |
| RSK-002 | Drone blocks critical route | Medium | High | Timeout, leash, safe alternate route, checkpoint | Repeated encounter softlock |
| RSK-003 | Asset import breaks skeleton/materials | Medium | Medium | Test representative asset early; document export preset | Import warnings or missing tracks |
| RSK-004 | Lighting/post-processing hurts readability | Medium | High | Readability review and low-quality preset test | Objective/hazard hard to identify |
| RSK-005 | Performance drops in final build | Medium | High | Profile early and after polish; budget transparencies/shadows | Frame time exceeds agreed target |
| RSK-006 | Audio masks gameplay cues | Low | Medium | Mix priorities, ducking, subtitle fallback | Critical cue missed in playtest |
